DESIGN BRIEF
Adapt the four supplied concept boards and the CIASecured visual direction to
ComplianceCore: navy navigation, teal actions, visible borderlines, organised
panels, simple forms and easy access. This remains a compliance application.
No implementation delivery terminology or consultancy workflow was added.

CHANGES
1. Shared navigation
The main sidebar now uses navy, a teal active item, compact grouped links and
the existing ComplianceCore branding. Organisation switching, role filtering,
settings, sign-out and the mobile drawer are retained. The desktop header has
a compliance breadcrumb, search, notifications and account access. The phone
header retains the organisation context and working search/navigation.

2. Shared page design
Page headers, actions, cards, filters, forms, dialogs and tables use a consistent
border, spacing and corner system. Bordered table headers and separated rows
make records easier to scan. Responsive table scrolling remains available.
Teal actions remain distinct from green success and amber/red attention states.
The theme lives in frontend/src/styles/compliance-concept.css and is loaded
after the existing shared styles. Tailwind's brand ramp matches the new theme.

3. Compliance journey
The existing recorded steps are grouped into Scope, Risk assessment,
Requirements and controls, Evidence and checks, and Review and reporting.
Each phase opens its steps; the selected phase and assessment persist in the
URL. The next required action remains directly accessible. A compact side
panel shows actual saved counts and assessment status. All steps remain
available through a full sequence list. People, training, suppliers, privacy,
incidents, assurance and monitoring stay linked in bordered workstream cards.
Phase completion counts come from the same saved readiness data as before.

4. Assessment workspace
Desktop: step rail on the left, the working record in the centre, and a separate
context/guidance panel on the right. On smaller screens these panels stack.
The scope preview reflects current form inputs and explicitly distinguishes
them from the saved assessment. Saved owner, review date and evidence counts
are shown separately. Guidance examples remain examples. Existing forms,
validation, saving and independent-review gates remain in place.

PRESERVATION
This change is confined to frontend presentation and assessment navigation.
No backend services, database schema, permission rules, approval requirements
or record formats were changed for this design adaptation. Existing worktree
changes were preserved. Document approval, evidence, control effectiveness
and final outcomes retain their separate meanings.
