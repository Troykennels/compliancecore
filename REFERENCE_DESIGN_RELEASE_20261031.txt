DESIGN
Applied reference-board proportions across shared headers, bordered cards,
filters, form controls, tables and navigation. This remains a compliance app.
The assessment desk has phase-grouped step navigation, individual labelled
fields, required/optional indicators, contextual explanations, and a separate
live scope preview. Collapsed phases remain accessible.

Risk worksheets focus the visible columns and highlight the selected row.
The context rail shows that record's saved asset, scenario, consequence,
assessment basis, owner, proposed treatment and justified remaining risk.
Existing row editing and validation remain available. Examples and suggestions
remain draft material; saved decisions are not inferred from the design PNGs.

The existing authenticator setup already supplies a QR code and manual key.
Sign-in verification now prevents duplicate six-digit submissions synchronously
and displays checking feedback. The backend authentication rules are unchanged.
