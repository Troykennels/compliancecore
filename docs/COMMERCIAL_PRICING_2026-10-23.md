# ComplianceCore commercial packages

Prepared for Orion Soft Limited, 23 October 2026. These are Orion's commercial
prices, not claimed competitor price quotations. Existing paid customer
agreements retain their stored renewal amounts and are not charged a new
onboarding fee by this rollout.

| Package | Monthly subscription | Annual prepayment (12 months) | One-time onboarding | First annual payment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter | ₦40,000 | ₦400,000 | ₦1,500,000 | ₦1,900,000 |
| Professional | ₦85,000 | ₦850,000 | ₦2,000,000 | ₦2,850,000 |
| Business | ₦180,000 | ₦1,800,000 | ₦3,500,000 | ₦5,300,000 |
| Enterprise, from | ₦480,000 | ₦4,800,000 | ₦5,000,000 minimum | ₦9,800,000 minimum |
| MSP / partner, from | ₦800,000 | ₦8,000,000 | ₦8,000,000 minimum | ₦16,000,000 minimum |

Annual prepayment buys 12 months for the price of 10: two months saved on the
subscription. Onboarding is paid once, together with the first subscription.
It is not discounted by the annual offer and never forms part of automatic
renewal consent. Checkout must show the full breakdown before redirecting to
Paystack. The server verifies the full amount and commits onboarding, the
access period and the invoice together. Abandoned checkout does not activate
access. Automatic renewal requires explicit consent and a reusable payment
method; bank-transfer customers can renew manually.

The Starter and Professional figures were set by the owner. Business preserves
the existing ₦180,000 monthly tier and prices the larger rollout above
Professional. Enterprise and MSP preserve the existing ₦480,000/₦800,000
subscription anchors and add progressively larger implementation minimums.
The higher fees reflect more stakeholders, locations, workflows, imported
records, validation and training work. They are commercial judgments; confirm
delivery effort and margin for each quotation.

Recommended onboarding deliverables: discovery and scope, organisation/team
configuration, agreed framework and control mapping, approved data import,
workflow configuration, user training, acceptance checks and handover. Agree
scope and dates in the contract. Third-party certification, statutory audit,
independent legal advice, custom integrations and ongoing managed compliance
services are separately quoted. SSO/SCIM must not be represented as already
implemented; the current release offers an integration enquiry, not a working
identity-provider connection.

Enterprise/MSP remain quotation-led. Starting prices are visible but a tailored
scope and written offer are required. Do not promise unlimited implementation
work, certifications or bespoke hosting for the minimum price. Any applicable
tax treatment must be stated in the commercial agreement and invoice; this
release does not invent an additional tax charge.

Research: [Vanta's official plans](https://www.vanta.com/pricing) and
[Drata's official plans](https://drata.com/plans) use personalised pricing and
differentiate framework coverage, organisational scale and enterprise
requirements. Neither supplies a public numeric rate card that establishes a
direct naira equivalent for ComplianceCore. Their published capabilities also
do not imply feature parity with this application.
