# C&I Leasing Plc presentation

Prepared by Orion Soft Limited. Open the existing ComplianceCore presentation workspace using the private Demo Access file. It remains a demonstration, with no operational integrations or real client records.

The company's [official overview](https://www.c-ileasing.com/company/) identifies fleet management, personnel outsourcing, telematics and marine services, with operations in several Nigerian cities. Its [service descriptions](https://www.c-ileasing.com/) explain fleet services, marine support, personnel services and Citracks asset visibility. These published activities informed the examples below. They do not establish that C&I has any of the sample risks or incidents.

## Ten-minute presentation

1. **Dashboard and the expandable presentation guide.** Explain that management can review work assigned to business-line owners in one workspace. All scores and completion rates are synthetic examples, not an assessment of C&I.
2. **Expiry → fleet document review.** Open “Sample fleet vehicle insurance review”. Show the due date and renewal owner. In Tasks, open “Review vehicle and crew certificate calendar”. In Evidence, download the fleet dispatch readiness checklist. Explain how ownership and dated evidence can reduce last-minute document chasing. No vehicle is dispatched or certified by this demonstration.
3. **Vendors → marine assurance.** Open “Example Marine Support Partner” and its assessment. Show the marine contractor audit and sample finding, then the marine supplier standard and pending approval. This demonstrates a review trail from supplier evidence to an accountable decision. This supplier is invented.
4. **Training → personnel outsourcing.** Open the privacy and payroll awareness programme. Compare assigned, in-progress and completed records. Open the personnel-data handling procedure and privacy risk example. Explain how HR and compliance could evidence access reviews and learning without exposing payroll data in a presentation.
5. **Incidents → Citracks tabletop.** Open the simulated account access investigation and its activity update. Follow the task to review account permissions, then download the Citracks user access review evidence. This demonstrates a documented decision and follow-up; there is no tracking-system integration or real breach.
6. **Reports and compliance score.** Show consolidated framework coverage and PDF/Excel exports. Explain that framework applicability and control effectiveness must be validated during implementation. ComplianceCore supports evidence and oversight; a sample score is not certification.
7. **Billing → sample invoice.** Download the first sample invoice. Point out the separate one-time onboarding and annual subscription rows. The annual example provides twelve months for ten monthly payments. Show a renewal sample: onboarding does not recur. Enterprise figures are illustrative starting prices, subject to an agreed scope; demo invoices cannot collect payment.

## Explain the value in plain language

| Team | Example question answered | Demonstrated outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Fleet operations | Who owns the upcoming document review? | Due date, owner, checklist and follow-up in one place |
| Marine operations | Has contractor evidence been reviewed? | Assessment, finding, action and approval trail |
| HR / outsourcing | Who still needs the privacy briefing? | Assigned learning and recorded completion evidence |
| Citracks / IT | How was the sample access concern handled? | Triage, decision, action and supporting review |
| Group management | Which actions need attention across business lines? | Consolidated reports with accountable owners |

These are proposed uses of existing modules. Agree actual workflows, document types, responsibilities, notification recipients, escalation rules and any integrations with C&I before operational deployment. The demo has no real staff, vehicle registrations, vessel names, customer data, live supplier connections, phone numbers or outgoing test alerts. Example scheduled reports and escalations are disabled. Device notifications require browser and operating-system permission and cannot override silent mode.
