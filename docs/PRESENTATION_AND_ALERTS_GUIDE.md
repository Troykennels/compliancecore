## A useful 12-minute demonstration

1. **Dashboard and compliance score:** explain the current control status, accountable owners, outstanding work and the illustrative historical trend. A readiness score is not external certification.
2. **Frameworks and controls:** open an adopted framework, then a control. Show implementation notes, assigned owner, due date and linked evidence. Explain how work changes the score.
3. **Evidence hub:** open a sample PDF; show versions, linked controls and audit history. Use only synthetic presentation files.
4. **Policies and approvals:** open the information security policy and the pending supplier standard review. Show the assigned reviewer and the separate, verifiable signature record.
5. **Risk register, vendors and incidents:** follow a risk through its mitigation, supplier assessment and incident update.
6. **Tasks, training, audits, calendar and expiry:** show the work assigned to staff and the upcoming dates. Escalation examples and scheduled demo reports are disabled to prevent unsolicited synthetic messages.
7. **Reports and analytics:** show framework coverage and download the executive PDF or spreadsheet. Explain what is measured and what requires management review.
8. **Team, API keys and webhooks:** show role boundaries. The sample API key is revoked and the webhook disabled. Third-party connections require separate configuration; do not describe an unconnected integration or SSO as working.
9. **Billing:** show the published monthly and annual subscriptions, one-time onboarding and first-payment breakdown. Demo invoices are draft examples and are not payable.

The account credentials are in the operator's private handover file under LocalAppData/OrionSoft. Do not put credentials in slides, source control or public marketing materials.

## Notification sound and alerts outside the app

1. Sign in on the device and open **Settings > Notifications**.
2. Click **Enable & test in-app sound**. This permission applies to this account and browser. A short chime confirms browser audio is available.
3. Click **Enable device alerts** and allow the browser permission. Registration must be saved successfully to the server before the app shows it as enabled.
4. On iPhone/iPad, add the site to the Home Screen and open it from that icon before enabling alerts. On Android or desktop, use a browser that supports Web Push.
5. Enable notifications and sounds for the browser/app in the device settings. Raise notification volume and check Silent mode, Do Not Disturb/Focus, battery restrictions and internet connectivity.
6. Use **Send test alert**, then switch to another app or close the ComplianceCore tab. Check the notification and click it to return to the intended page.

Foreground chimes use browser audio; background alerts use persistent Web Push and the operating system's notification sound. A website cannot override Silent/Focus or guarantee a custom sound while closed. Force-stopping a browser, blocking notifications or removing network access can prevent delivery. No customer device has been certified merely by an automated service-worker test: perform this short check on the actual presentation phone.

Reference: [MDN notification options](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/ServiceWorkerRegistration/showNotification), [browser autoplay permissions](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Media/Guides/Autoplay), [Apple Web Push](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/sending-web-push-notifications-in-web-apps-and-browsers).
